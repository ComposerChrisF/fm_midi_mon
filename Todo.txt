=== LinnStrument
===================================================================================================
- Convert incoming MIDI streams into:
    - All current CC/aftertouch/etc. values (per channel)
    - Current notes on (per channel)
    - Current effective pitch bend (in 1/2 steps, based on map of input's PW max bend) (per channel)
    - Apply bend to current notes on (and tails of note offs, too) (per channel)
- Given current params:
    - Adjust per-note (really per-channel for MPE) bend to account for pitch magnets (that are 
    perhaps further adjusted for JI)
    - NOTE: All "windows" below, will need to have a certain hysteresis.
    - Pause-based magnet: Once a pitch is within window (default is 1/4 step each direction) for
    at least x seconds, the average pitch is adjusted to the magnet pitch.  Must still allow for
    vibrato, so will need to tweak this algorithm (and perhaps have bool switch to account or
    ignore vibrato).
    - Glissando-based magnet (i.e. "fretted"): magnet activates immediately, based on being within
    window.
- Output MIDI based on output's PW max bend (per channel)
